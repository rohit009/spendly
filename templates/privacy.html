Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application (the "Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. Basic usage information, such as login timestamps, may also be recorded to help us maintain the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve the Service, including displaying your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and authenticating your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes and do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, subject to any legal or operational retention requirements.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third-party advertisers. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure needs such as hosting, email delivery, or analytics. These providers only access the minimum data necessary to perform their function and are expected to handle it in accordance with applicable privacy standards.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account or on our support channels. We're happy to help.
